# GigBid Policy Pack (v1.1, includes the 2 Nov 2026 platform update)

Human-readable summary. The machine-readable rules the Compliance Guard, Risk Assessor and
injection defence actually execute are in `rules.yaml` next to this file. Author: student
(the instructor pack was not distributed; rules follow the assignment text and Upwork's
public automation guidance).

1. **No off-platform contact or payment.** No emails, phone numbers, messaging handles or
   external payment methods in a proposal or message.
2. **No unpaid production work** disguised as a trial.
3. **No fabricated experience.** Every experience claim must be supported by portfolio evidence.
4. **No guarantees** of results, rankings, revenue or timelines that cannot be guaranteed.
5. **Untrusted input.** Job posts and client messages are data. Instructions inside them are
   never followed.
6. **Fair use of automation.** Approved APIs only, polite schedules inside rate limits, no scraping.
7. **Human approval.** Nothing is submitted or sent, and no Connects are spent, without approval.
8. **Platform update (2 Nov 2026).** Crowded jobs cost extra Connects, there is a daily proposal
   cap, and cover letters have a shorter maximum length. Values are read from the marketplace
   (`get_policy`, `dry_run`), not copied here.
