# Store demand forecasting

## Context
A retail client needed weekly demand forecasts for 320 products across 18 stores.

## What I did
- Predicted next quarter's demand from seasonal history.
- Pandas.
- Python.
- Tracked runs and promoted models through a registry.

## Result
The project reduced stock-outs by 18% and forecast error (MAPE) from 27% to 14%.
