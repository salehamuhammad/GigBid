# Support knowledge assistant

## Context
A saas client needed a question-answering assistant over 4,200 help-centre articles for the support team.

## What I did
- RAG.
- Wired the model calls into a stateful graph of cooperating agents.
- An approximate nearest-neighbour index over embeddings.
- FastAPI.
- Scripted in the usual data-science language.

## Result
The project cut median first-response time from 11 minutes to 4 and deflected 31% of tickets.
