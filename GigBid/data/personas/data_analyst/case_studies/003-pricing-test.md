# Pricing page experiment

## Context
A e-commerce client needed a three-arm pricing test with a pre-registered analysis plan.

## What I did
- Ran controlled experiments and checked significance.
- Confidence intervals and regression instead of eyeballing charts.
- Python.
- Web-traffic and funnel events from Google's analytics product.

## Result
