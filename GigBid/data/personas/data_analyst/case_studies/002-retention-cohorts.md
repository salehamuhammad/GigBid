# Subscriber retention study

## Context
A media client needed monthly retention curves by acquisition channel.

## What I did
- Tracked how each signup month retained over time.
- Wrote the joins and window functions by hand.
- Dataframes.
- Python.
- Data visualization.

## Result
The project identified a channel with 2x churn, saving $180k in ad spend.
